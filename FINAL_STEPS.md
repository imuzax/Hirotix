# 🚀 Hirotix Final Startup Guide (Easy Mode)

Follow these 4 steps every time you want to start the project. Do them in this exact order:

### **Step 1: Start MySQL**
- Open **XAMPP Control Panel**.
- Click **Start** for MySQL.
- (Ensure port 3306 is green).

### **Step 2: Start Java Backend**
- Open your project in **IntelliJ IDEA**.
- Find `HirotixBackendApplication.java`.
- Click the **Green Play Button** (Run).
- Wait for `Started HirotixBackendApplication` in the console.

### **Step 3: Start AI Engine**
- Go to the project folder: `D:\Projects\College\Hirotix`.
- **Double-click `START_HIROTIX.bat`**.
- Wait for the message: **`SUCCESS: HIROTIX AI IS ONLINE!`**.
- *Leave this window open in the background.*

### **Step 4: Start Frontend**
- Open the project in **VS Code**.
- Right-click `index.html` and click **"Open with Live Server"**.
- (Or click the "Go Live" button at the bottom right).

---

### **✅ Troubleshooting (If AI shows "Offline")**
1. Refresh your browser page (F5).
2. If it still shows offline, close the AI window from **Step 3** and run `START_HIROTIX.bat` again.
3. Check that your internet is connected (needed for the Groq AI API).
