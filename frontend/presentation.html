Introduction

What is Hirotix?

Hirotix is a Smart Job Website.

Normally, finding a job or hiring someone is very slow. Companies read resumes manually.

We use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to connect students with companies automatically and quickly.

The Problem

The Old Way of Hiring

Why is hiring so difficult today?

For Students

It is very hard to find the right job. Good students get rejected just because their resume misses some keywords.

For Companies

HR Managers have to read hundreds of resumes by hand. It takes too much time and they make mistakes.

The Objective

Our Goal

What we want to achieve with Hirotix

1. Match Automatically

Use AI to match the student's skills with the company's job instantly.

2. Help Students Learn

Tell students what skills they are missing, so they can learn them.

3. Save Time

Make the process super fast. From weeks to just a few minutes!

The Solution

How Hirotix Solves It

  • 1
    You just upload your resume once.
  • 2
    AI reads it and understands your skills automatically.
  • 3
    AI finds the best jobs for you without searching.
  • 4
    Companies get a ready list of the best students.
Key Functionality

Main Features

AI Matching

Finds the perfect job using smart AI, not just dumb keywords.

Skill Checker

Tells you exactly what you need to learn to get your dream job.

Instant Apply

No long forms. Apply to any job with just one single click.

Auto Resume Reading

AI extracts your details automatically. No typing needed.

Under the hood

Technologies Used

Frontend

HTML5, CSS3, JS

Backend

Java Spring Boot & MySQL

Handles the secure logic, REST APIs, and database.

AI Brain

Python Flask & Groq API

Powers the LLM (Llama-3) for smart resume matching.

Conclusion

Hirotix makes hiring Very Fast and Very Easy.

It helps students get their dream jobs, and helps companies find the best talent without wasting time.
